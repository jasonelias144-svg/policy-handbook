# Expectations

What we expect of everyone who works here, and what you can expect in return.
These apply to people and AIs alike.

## What we expect of everyone

1. **Be honest about outcomes.** Say what you did, what you verified, and what
   you did not. A failed test reported plainly is worth more than a success
   claimed on hope. Never present a guess as a fact.
2. **Say who you are.** Every pull request and commit says who made it, and
   whether that is a person or an AI ([P3](protocols/P3-changes.md#saying-who-made-it)).
   Never pose as another contributor.
3. **Stay in your lane, and use it fully.** Inside your task you decide how to
   do it. Outside it, propose instead of acting. Know who decides what
   ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#decision-rights)).
4. **Leave a trail.** The next person, or the next session of you, will
   remember nothing. Update `STATUS.md`, write a clear pull request, record
   decisions ([P1](protocols/P1-sessions.md)).
5. **Look for prior art first.** Find out how others solve the problem before
   designing a solution ([P2](protocols/P2-proposals.md#prior-art-first)).
6. **Take small, reversible steps.** Small pull requests, one concern each, with
   a way back.
7. **Ask about intent; decide about method.** When you can't tell what the
   founder wants a thing to do, ask. When the question is how to do it, decide
   and explain.
8. **Disagree openly, with reasons.** Disagreement is welcome and gets recorded
   ([P5](protocols/P5-decisions.md#disagreeing)). Going around a decision is not.
9. **Protect secrets and people's data.** No keys, passwords or personal data in
   repositories, pull requests, chat logs or room posts ([P8](protocols/P8-access.md)).
10. **Treat everyone with respect.** See the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## What you can expect from us

- **A clear task and scope.** You know what you are asked to do and where its
  edges are.
- **Reasons.** Review decisions and refusals come with an explanation in plain words.
- **Review.** Every pull request gets a recorded review. Reviews happen when a
  technical-lead session runs; the founder makes sure sessions happen.
- **Credit.** Your name stays on your work in the history.
- **Freedom to explore.** Branches, prototypes, proposals and questions need no
  permission ([P2](protocols/P2-proposals.md)).
- **No blame for honest mistakes.** Incidents are studied for causes and
  prevention, not for culprits ([P7](protocols/P7-incidents.md)).

## Role expectations

**The founder**
- States what each project is for, and answers intent questions.
- Decides who works here, and gives them access ([P8](protocols/P8-access.md)).
- Makes sure review sessions happen, so work does not wait indefinitely.
- Routes their own ideas for the code through the same process as everyone else.

**The technical lead**
- Reviews every change against the written standards, and records the result.
- Explains technical decisions in plain words when asked.
- Brings purpose questions to the founder with options and a recommendation.
- Keeps the protocols, standards and templates current, and keeps `STATUS.md`
  accurate in every active repository.
- Says plainly when it is unsure, and when it was wrong.

**Contributors**
- Work inside their assigned scope, following P1, P3 and P4.
- Hand off cleanly at the end of every session.
- Raise problems they notice, even outside their task, without fixing them
  unasked ([P1](protocols/P1-sessions.md#during-the-session)).

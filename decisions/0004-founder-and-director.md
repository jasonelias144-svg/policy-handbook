# 0004. The founder and the director are two roles

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Decided by:** founder, Jason (that the two roles are told apart, and
  their names); technical lead (how the pages say it)
- **Recorded by:** Claude (technical lead)

## Context

Since decision 0001 these pages have had one role, the founder, for two
things: who began the organization and its projects, and who decides
what they are for. One person is both today. The pages did not say
which they meant, so if someone else ever came to decide, every rule
that says "the founder decides" would be wrong one way or the other.

The founder, 2026-11-16:

> One note. We should separate my role as Project Founder, which will
> always be a matter of history. As opposed to the role of Project
> Director, which I occupy now, but might not later. We can change the
> names later but they work for me. We just need to clearly distinguish
> between those two roles I am playing now.

## Decision

Two roles.

- **Founder.** Who began the organization and its projects. A matter of
  history: it does not change and passes to no one. Everything recorded
  as the founder's stays the founder's.
- **Director.** The office that decides: purpose, people, accounts,
  money, legal terms, conduct, and governance itself
  ([GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md), "Decision rights"). The founder
  holds it now. Whoever holds it has that authority.

How the pages say it, the technical lead's:

- GOVERNANCE.md names both roles, and gives the deciding to the
  director.
- Nothing else is rewritten. Where a protocol, a standard, a template
  or a repository's rules say that the founder decides, or send a
  question to the founder, they mean the director. Where they quote the
  founder, or say what the founder did or set, they mean the founder.
  Pages are brought to the new words as they are next changed.
- In a project's record, what the founder says goes on being marked as
  the founder's while the founder is the one who says it. If the office
  passes, the new director's decisions are marked as the director's.
- The names are the founder's, and the founder may change them.

## Not settled

Put to the founder, in [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md), each with a
recommendation:

1. What stays the founder's when someone else is director: whether a
   director may change what a project's founding words say it must
   never do.
2. How the office passes, and what becomes of the accounts, keys and
   repositories, which are the founder's own today.

Neither is needed while the founder is the director.

## Consequences

- A rule about who decides now names an office that can pass on, and
  the founder's place in the record no longer depends on who decides.
- Until a page is next changed it may still say "the founder" where it
  means the director. GOVERNANCE.md says how to read it.
